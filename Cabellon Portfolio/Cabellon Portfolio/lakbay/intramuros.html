History of Intramuros

Intramuros, also known as the "Walled City," is the oldest district and historic core of Manila, the capital of the Philippines. Built by the Spanish colonial government in the late 16th century, Intramuros served as the center of political, religious, and military power during the Spanish colonial period.

The district is surrounded by massive stone walls and fortifications, which were designed to protect the city from invasions. Intramuros has a rich history, with landmarks such as Fort Santiago, San Agustin Church, and Manila Cathedral showcasing its colonial heritage.
