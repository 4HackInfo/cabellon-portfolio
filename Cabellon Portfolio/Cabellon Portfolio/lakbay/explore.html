Magellan's Cross, Cebu

Cebu offers a mix of cultural and natural attractions, from historic landmarks to beautiful beaches. Visit the famous Magellan's Cross, explore underwater ecosystems, or hike to breathtaking viewpoints.

Explore More

Boracay Island, Aklan

Boracay Island is known for its stunning white sand beaches, crystal-clear waters, and vibrant nightlife. It's a popular destination for beach lovers and water sports enthusiasts.

El Nido, Palawan

Palawan is a paradise for nature lovers, with its pristine beaches, towering limestone cliffs, and lush jungles. Explore underground rivers, go island hopping, or simply relax on the beach.

Explore More

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

Bohol is famous for the Chocolate Hills, unique geological formations. Bohol offers other attractions like exploring the Loboc River, spotting tarsiers at the Tarsier Conservation Area, and visiting historical sites like the Baclayon Church.

Explore More

Banaue Rice Terraces, Ifugao

The Banaue Rice Terraces are a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the most popular tourist destinations in the Philippines. The terraces are carved into the mountainsides and are said to be over 2,000 years old. Visitors can go trekking or visit nearby villages to learn more about the Ifugao culture.

Explore More

Siargao Island, Surigao del Norte

Siargao is a tear-shaped island known for its world-class surfing. The island is also home to beautiful beaches, lagoons, and waterfalls. Siargao offers activities like island hopping, spelunking at the Magpupungko Rock Pools, and exploring the Sugba Lagoon.

Explore More

Vigan Heritage City, Ilocos Sur

Vigan is a UNESCO World Heritage City and is known for its well-preserved Spanish colonial architecture. Visitors can take a walk down Calle Crisologo, the main street in Vigan, which is lined with Spanish colonial houses, ride a kalesa (horse-drawn carriage), and explore historical landmarks like the Syquia Mansion and Crisologo Museum.

Explore More

Mayon Volcano, Legazpi, Albay

Mayon Volcano is a near-perfect cone-shaped active volcano, considered one of the most beautiful volcanoes in the world. Towering over the surrounding landscape, Mayon Volcano offers breathtaking views and a unique opportunity to witness the raw power of nature.

Explore More

Puerto Princesa Underground River, Palawan

The Puerto Princesa Underground River is a UNESCO World Heritage Site and one of the longest underground rivers globally. Visitors can embark on a mesmerizing boat ride through the river, meandering through a cave system adorned with enchanting stalactites and stalagmites.

Explore More

Intramuros, Manila

This walled city built by the Spanish in the 16th century is a must-visit for history buffs. Explore Fort Santiago, wander along cobblestone streets, visit historical landmarks like San Agustin Church and Casa Manila, and soak in the unique atmosphere.
