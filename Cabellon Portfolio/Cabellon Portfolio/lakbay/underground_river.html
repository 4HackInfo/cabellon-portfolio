Exploring the Underground River

The Puerto Princesa Subterranean River National Park, also known as the Underground River, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site located in the province of Palawan, Philippines. It features a spectacular limestone karst landscape with an underground river flowing through a cave system that extends for more than 24 kilometers.

The Underground River is one of the New Seven Wonders of Nature and is home to a diverse range of flora and fauna, including several endemic species. Visitors can explore the river by boat, marveling at its stunning rock formations and the eerie beauty of the underground world.
