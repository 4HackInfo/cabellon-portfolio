History of Bohol

Bohol is an island province in the Central Visayas region of the Philippines known for its natural attractions and historical landmarks. The province is famous for its unique geological formations, such as the Chocolate Hills, which are cone-shaped hills covered in grass that turn brown during the dry season, resembling chocolate kisses.

Bohol's history is rich and diverse, with evidence of human habitation dating back thousands of years. The province was once part of the ancient Rajahnate of Butuan and later became a stronghold of the Spanish colonial government.
