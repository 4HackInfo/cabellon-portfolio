History of Cebu

Cebu is a province of the Philippines located in the Central Visayas region. It is one of the oldest and most prosperous areas in the country, with a rich history dating back to pre-colonial times. Cebu was the site of the famous encounter between Portuguese explorer Ferdinand Magellan and local chieftain Rajah Humabon in 1521, marking the beginning of Spanish colonization in the Philippines.

Throughout its history, Cebu has played a significant role in Philippine trade, culture, and politics. Today, it is a vibrant destination known for its beautiful beaches, historical landmarks, and rich cultural heritage.
