History of Boracay

Boracay is a small island located in the Visayas region of the Philippines. It was once a hidden gem known only to locals until it gained international fame for its stunning white sand beaches and crystal-clear waters. In recent years, Boracay has become a popular tourist destination, attracting visitors from all over the world.

The island's history dates back to ancient times when it was inhabited by indigenous Austronesian tribes. Today, Boracay is known for its vibrant nightlife, water sports activities, and breathtaking sunsets.
